## Cacao — Myoelectric Hand Prototype

Cacao is MOVE Prosthetics’ myoelectric hand prototype. Surface EMG signals from two forearm muscles are amplified and digitised by an **ADS1299-4** front-end, then processed by an **STM32H753ZI** to infer open/close intent. A **DRV8838-driven gearmotor** executes the command by pulling a tendon through a 3D-printed prosthetic hand.

The prototype is a **development and teaching platform**, not a certified medical device. Success means reliable signal-to-grip operation that students can reproduce and build upon.
